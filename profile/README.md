SetKernel Digital Inc. is a small team of senior engineers in Halifax. Since 2022 we have helped
small businesses across Atlantic Canada, Canada and the United States. We deliver the AI work and the
IT, security, web and cloud foundation it runs on, so clients deal with one team instead of several
vendors.

### What we do
- **AI that's useful day to day.** AI receptionists, workflow automation, rollouts of Copilot, ChatGPT
  and Claude, and private AI built on your own documents.
- **Managed IT and Microsoft 365.** Devices, identity, email and support, done properly.
- **Cybersecurity.** Hardening, access control and secure configuration.
- **Cloud and infrastructure.** Cloudflare, networking and infrastructure as code.
- **Web, SEO and custom software.** Fast websites, visibility in search and AI answers, and software built to order.

### How we build
Our engineers work alongside AI agents. Every change goes through a pull request and a review, and a
person approves it before it ships. Infrastructure is defined as code and planned before it's applied.
